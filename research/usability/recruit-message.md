# Recruitment message for the §17.3 usability test

> Paste this into WhatsApp, or send it as an email. It asks for **five students who are not squad
> members** and books **25 minutes each**. It is written so that a participant knows what they are
> agreeing to before they arrive, which is what the consent sheet then confirms in writing.

---

**Subject: 25 minutes to test a study app, this week?**

Hi,

We are building **StudyForge**, an iPhone app that turns your lecture material into summaries,
flashcards and quizzes. It is an IT8108 project and we need it tested by real students before we
submit, which is where you come in.

What it involves: **25 minutes**, one to one, on a phone we provide. You will be asked to do six
things with the app, one at a time, while saying out loud what you are looking for. At the end
there are ten short questions about how it felt. There are no right answers, and nothing you do
can break it.

What it is not: it is not a test of you, it is not marked, and it will not affect your grades.

What we need from you: **one slot**, and please bring nothing with you. If you study anything at
all, you are exactly who we need. We are looking for five people, and the first five slots we can
fill are the ones we will run.

You will be asked to sign a one-page consent sheet when you arrive. It says what we record (task
success, how long each task took, anything confusing you say) and what we do not (your name, your
marks, and any of your own study material). You can stop at any point.

Slots: **\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_** (we will confirm by reply).

Thanks,
Saleh, on behalf of the StudyForge team

## Facilitator: what to do the moment a slot is confirmed

1. Write the slot in the table below, and tell the participant where to meet you.
2. Print one copy of **Part 2 (consent)**, **Part 3 (SUS)** and **Part 4 (log sheet)** from
   `StudyForge-Usability-Packet.docx` for that participant.
3. Install the build on the test device and walk the six tasks yourself once. Do not run a
   session on a build you have not walked that day.
4. Put a 5 to 20 page PDF on the device for task T2.

## If five 25-minute sessions do not fit the time left

The method is 25 minutes per participant, which is 2 hours 10 minutes of sessions alone. Two
honest ways to fit a shorter window, both of which must be written up as they were actually run:

- **Run 15-minute sessions**: consent and warm-up 3 minutes, the six tasks 9 minutes, the SUS and
  debrief 3 minutes. The SUS is unaffected by session length, so the score stays comparable. The
  time on task still measures what it measures.
- **Two facilitators, two devices, two rooms**: sessions still moderated one to one, which is what
  the protocol protects. Record which facilitator ran which session.

What must **not** change: the do-not-help rule, the 90-second fail rule, the exact wording of the
six tasks, and the requirement that no participant is a squad member. A rescued participant
produces no finding, and five people who were told what to tap produce no data at all.
