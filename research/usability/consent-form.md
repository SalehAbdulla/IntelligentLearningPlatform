# Participant information and consent

> One copy per participant. The participant reads it, asks anything they want, and signs.
> **A participant who does not sign does not take part.**

## Participant information

**What this is.** A student project at Bahrain Polytechnic is building a study app that turns a
student's own course material into summaries, flashcards and quizzes. Before it is finished, we need
to watch people use it, so we can find what is confusing while there is still time to fix it.

**What you will be asked to do.** Spend about **25 minutes** using the app while someone watches and
takes notes. You will be asked to create an account, add a document, and generate and study study
material from it. You will be asked to **think aloud** — to say what you are looking for and what
you expect to happen. Afterwards you will answer ten short statements about the app.

**You are testing the app, not being tested.** There are no wrong answers and nothing you do is
recorded as a judgement of you. If you cannot work something out, that is a finding we need, not a
mistake on your part.

**What is recorded.**

- Whether each task was completed, and roughly how long it took
- Notes on where you hesitated, and what you said about it
- Your answers to the ten statements

**What is *not* recorded.**

- Your name, student ID or contact details, in any file the team keeps
- Your email address or the account you create for the test
- **Any of your own study material** — you will be given a test document to use, not asked for yours
- Audio or video of you

**Anonymity.** You are identified in our notes and in the report only as **P1, P2, P3, P4 or P5**.
The notes do not connect a participant number to a person. Nobody outside the project team sees the
raw notes.

**How the results are used.** They go into a section of our Design Document, which is read by our
module tutor. The report records the task results, a usability score, and the problems we found —
**including the problems**, because those are what we need.

**Taking part is voluntary.** You may stop at any time, for any reason, without giving one, and you
may ask for your notes to be destroyed afterwards. There is no penalty of any kind, and it will not
affect your studies or your relationship with the team.

**Your rights.** This test follows the **Bahrain Personal Data Protection Law (2018)** and the
project's own privacy commitments. You may ask to see, correct or delete anything recorded about
you, at any time, by contacting the person named below.

**Contact.** Saleh Abdulla, project lead, Bahrain Polytechnic — `202300540` at student.bp.edu.bh.
