# System Usability Scale responses

> **Where the session is recorded.** One line per participant, the ten SUS responses in order,
> items 1 to 10, each a value from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). Add the task rows
> if you have them, five marks of `P` (pass) or `F` (fail) per task, in participant order.
>
> Then run: `python3 tools/usability-score.py research/usability/responses.md`
>
> It prints the participant table, the SUS score with its band, the task table and the paragraph
> for §17.3, scored exactly as Brooke (1996) defines it. Nothing is written to any file.
>
> **Record what actually happened.** A failed task is a finding, not an embarrassment. Never
> round a score, and never add a participant who did not take part.
